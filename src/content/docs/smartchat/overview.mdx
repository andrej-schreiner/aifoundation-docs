SmartChat is a managed, sovereign offering for AI-assisted knowledge work — featuring chat with your documents, deep research, and data and tool integrations. Built on T-Systems AI Foundation Services and operated on T-Cloud, which is certified to BSI-C5 and ISO 27017/27018, it delivers **GDPR** compliance, audit logging, role-based access, and a contractually agreed service level as part of the product rather than leaving them as your team's homework.

In practice, SmartChat is what you reach for when the chat UI is the easy part and the hard part is running it safely for regulated workloads: **healthcare**, **public sector**, **finance**, or any context where data residency, professional secrecy, and operational accountability matter most.

## Key Features

- **End-to-End Hosting** — Fully managed document knowledge bases with no infrastructure to maintain
- **Enterprise RAG** — Upload documents and query them via UI or API with over 50 retrieval settings
- **Multi-Tenancy** — Isolated spaces with strict data separation for different confidentiality levels and organizational boundaries
- **Compliance** — GDPR-compliant, running on T-Cloud infrastructure that is certified to BSI-C5 and ISO 27017/27018 ([T-Cloud certificates](https://www.t-systems.com/de/en/cloud-services/public-cloud/certificates-public-cloud)). Includes audit logging and role-based access control at the product level
- **Enterprise Security** — OWASP Top 10 hardened, penetration-tested by Deutsche Telekom security professionals, with enterprise SSO and role-based access control
- **Fully Sovereign** — European across the entire stack — the application, the cloud, the LLMs, and the infrastructure are all operated by T-Systems in Germany
- **Operational Support** — Provided by T-Systems with contractually agreed service levels and service hours
- **Predictable Pricing** — Monthly flat-rate fees

## Getting Started

To use SmartChat, contact the AIFS team at **[ai@t-systems.com](mailto:ai@t-systems.com)** for access credentials. Once set up, you can:

1. Authenticate via the API
2. Upload and ingest documents
3. Create chat sessions
4. Query your documents with AI

### Available Models

SmartChat is operated by T-Systems with a curated model selection from the **AI Foundation Services**. Models running fully in T-Cloud Germany include:

- **Llama 3.3 70B** — general chat and reasoning
- **GPT-OSS 120B** — largest open-weight model hosted in T-Cloud Germany
- **Qwen3-Next 80B** — long-context, reasoning
- **Gemma 4** — new, preview
- **Mistral Small 3** — European-developed
- **BGE-M3** and **Jina Embeddings v2 Base DE** — embeddings for multilingual and German-language RAG
- **Whisper Large v3** — speech-to-text

EU-hosted models (GPT-5.2, Claude 4.6, Gemini 3 Pro and others) are available on request. Full AIFS model list: [docs.llmhub.t-systems.net/getting-started/models](https://docs.llmhub.t-systems.net/getting-started/models/).
