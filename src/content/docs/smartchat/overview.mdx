SmartChat is a managed, sovereign offering for AI-assisted knowledge work — featuring chat with your documents, deep research, and data and tool integrations. Built on T-Systems AI Foundation Services and operated on T-Cloud, it delivers **GDPR**, **BSI-C5**, and **ISO 27017/27018** compliance, audit logging, role-based access, and an SLA as part of the product rather than leaving them as your team's homework.

In practice, SmartChat is what you reach for when the chat UI is the easy part and the hard part is running it safely for regulated workloads: **healthcare**, **public sector**, **finance**, or any context where data residency, professional secrecy, and operational accountability matter most.

## Key Features

- **End-to-End Hosting** — Fully managed document knowledge bases with no infrastructure to maintain
- **Enterprise RAG** — Upload documents and query them via UI or API with over 50 retrieval settings
- **Multi-Tenancy** — Isolated spaces with strict data separation for different confidentiality levels and organizational boundaries
- **Compliance** — **GDPR**, **BSI-C5**, and **ISO 27017/27018** certified, with audit logging — built for regulated workloads in **healthcare**, **public sector**, and **finance**
- **Enterprise Security** — OWASP Top 10 hardened, penetration-tested by Deutsche Telekom security professionals, with enterprise SSO and role-based access control
- **Fully Sovereign** — European across the entire stack — the application, the cloud, the LLMs, and the infrastructure are all operated by T-Systems in Germany
- **Operational Support** — Dedicated support during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9:00–17:00 CET/CEST) with SLA
- **Predictable Pricing** — Monthly flat-rate fees

## Getting Started

To use SmartChat, contact the AIFS team at **[ai@t-systems.com](mailto:ai@t-systems.com)** for access credentials. Once set up, you can:

1. Authenticate via the API
2. Upload and ingest documents
3. Create chat sessions
4. Query your documents with AI
