---
title: "Deprecations"
description: "Models scheduled for retirement and models already retired from AI Foundation Services, with dates and replacements."
---

Models leave the catalog on a published date. Until that date, migrate to the replacement. After it, requests to the old model ID return an error that names the replacement.

Major changes are also announced on the [Changelog](/changelog).

## Upcoming

| Provider | Model | Retirement date | Suggested replacement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google | Gemini 3 Flash | 2026-11-30 | Gemini 3.8 Flash |

:::caution[Important]
If you use Gemini 3 Flash, migrate to Gemini 3.8 Flash before **2026-11-30**.
:::
