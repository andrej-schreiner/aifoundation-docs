AI Foundation Services gives you a single OpenAI-compatible API across two clearly separated categories of models — those operated by Telekom in the EU/EEA, and those forwarded to third-party providers under their own terms. This page summarizes the trust and compliance posture; the [Service Description (PDF)](/files/Leistungsbeschreibung_LLM_Serving-Service_EN.pdf) is the binding source.

:::tip[No training on customer data]
For every model hosted by Telekom, **customer prompts and responses are not permanently stored and are never used to train or fine-tune any model**. Prefix caching keeps processed requests for a limited time so repeat requests are answered faster; it is on by default and can be switched off. See [Prefix caching](/guides/prefix-caching).
:::


## Two Model Categories — Pick the Right One for Your Data

The Service Description splits the catalog into two categories with materially different data-sovereignty profiles. **Selecting a model is selecting its data-handling regime.**

### Category 1 — EU/EEA & adequacy-decision processing

Operated by Telekom on T-Cloud Public. Examples:

- Google Gemma 4 31B
- Mistral Small 4
- Alibaba Qwen 3.6 35B
- Alibaba Qwen 3.8 27B
- NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Super
- Zhipu AI GLM 5.3
- Zhipu AI GLM 5.2
- Zhipu AI GLM 5.3 Flash
- OpenAI GPT-OSS-120B
- OpenAI Whisper Large v3 / v3 Turbo (STT)
- Jina embeddings v2, BAAI BGE m3, Qwen3 Embedding 8B (embeddings)

For these models:

- Data processing happens **inside the EU/EEA or in countries with an EU adequacy decision**
- Customer prompts and returned results are **not permanently stored** and **not viewable** by Telekom or third parties

### Category 2 — Third-party providers, sub-processors worldwide

Forwarded by Telekom directly to the third-party provider, who runs the model on its own infrastructure. Examples:

- OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-5.6 Terra, and GPT-5.6 Luna — on Microsoft Azure (Sweden)
- OpenAI GPT-6 Luna — on Microsoft Azure (Sweden)
- OpenAI GPT-6.1 Sol — on Microsoft Azure (worldwide)
- OpenAI GPT Image 2 — on Microsoft Azure (Sweden)
- OpenAI Ada Text Embedding — on Microsoft Azure (France)
- Anthropic Claude Sonnet 5, Claude Sonnet 5.5, Claude Opus 5, Claude Opus 5.5, and Claude Fable 5 — on Google Cloud (europe-west1) or Microsoft Azure
- Google Gemini 3.1 Pro, Gemini 3.5 Flash, Gemini 3.8 Flash, and Gemini 3 Pro Image — on Google Cloud (worldwide)

For these models:

- The third-party provider may operate **sub-processors worldwide**, so customer data may be processed worldwide
- By selecting a Category 2 model, the customer **accepts the third-party provider's terms, license, and data-protection policies**
- Outages of these third-party providers are excused from Telekom's [availability calculation](/reference/service-levels#excused-events)

:::caution
Certain data categories (for example social or health data under German law) **may not legally be processed outside the EU/EEA**. Reviewing the legal basis for using a specific model is the customer's sole responsibility.
:::


## EU AI Act Alignment

- Telekom delivers the service in line with its applicable obligations under [Regulation (EU) 2024/1689](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R1689) (the AI Act).
- The customer is contractually required to use the service in compliance with the AI Act.
- The service is **not designed or permitted for high-risk AI systems** as defined by the AI Act, nor for any prohibited use case.
- The service is **not a medical device** under MDR and may not be used for diagnosis, prevention, monitoring, prediction, prognosis, treatment, or alleviation of disease.

## GDPR & Data Processing

- Category 1 models run on GDPR-compliant EU hosting on T-Cloud Public ([EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)). For Category 2 models, the third-party provider's data-protection terms apply.
- A **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** based on Telekom's template is available on request — required if the customer processes personal data through the service.
- Telekom acts as a data processor; the customer remains the data controller.
- The customer is responsible for confirming that the data they submit is lawful to process, including processing-location restrictions.


## Data Handling Summary

| Aspect | Category 1 (EU-operated) | Category 2 (Third-party) |
|---|---|---|
| **Processing location** | EU/EEA or adequacy-decision country | Determined by third-party provider; may be worldwide |
| **Storage** | Not permanently stored (short-lived prefix cache, can be switched off) | Per third-party provider's terms |
| **Visibility** | Not viewable by Telekom or third parties | Per third-party provider's terms |
| **Training use** | Not used for model training | Not used for model training |
| **Encryption in transit** | TLS-secured (HTTPS) | TLS-secured (HTTPS) |
| **Authentication** | API keys via [Self-Service Portal](https://apikey.llmhub.t-systems.net) | API keys via [Self-Service Portal](https://apikey.llmhub.t-systems.net) |


## Termination & Data Deletion

The service can be terminated with one month's notice to month-end. **Upon termination, all access is deactivated and customer data is deleted.** See [Service Levels: Termination](/reference/service-levels#termination).
