## Plans

The minimum is a monthly token revenue commitment. Usage above it is billed at list prices. Plans differ by throughput (TPM and RPM) and by Premium model access. Premium models such as Claude Opus are included on Professional and Agentic, not on Essential.

- **Essential** — €1,000 / month. Standard catalog. Lower throughput.
- **Professional** — €3,000 / month. Premium models included. Medium throughput.
- **Agentic** — €5,000 / month. Premium models included. Highest best-effort throughput.
- **Enterprise** — Custom token commitment and custom rate limits.
