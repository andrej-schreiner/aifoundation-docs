## What are the AI Foundation Services?

The AI Foundation Services by <span class="nowrap">T-Systems</span> offer companies a secure platform for developing and scaling AI solutions with LLMs. They enable the use of 30+ language models — including open-source models like Mistral, Qwen, and Gemma — as well as ready-made services for RAG. The open-source models run on the GDPR-compliant T-Cloud in Germany; proprietary models are forwarded to Microsoft Azure or Google Cloud. See [Enterprise Trust](/reference/enterprise-trust).

## Which LLMs are available?

The selection is continuously updated with both open- and closed-source models, including Mistral, Qwen, OpenAI GPT, and Claude Sonnet. <span class="nowrap">T-Systems</span> offers unimodal and multimodal models that can process text, image, and audio. See [Available Models](/models) for the current list.

## On which cloud platform is the hosting provided?

Open-source models are operated by <span class="nowrap">T-Systems</span> on the GDPR-compliant T-Cloud in Germany. Closed-source models are forwarded to their upstream provider on Microsoft Azure or Google Cloud — see [Enterprise Trust](/reference/enterprise-trust) for the full data-handling model.

## Can I test the AI Foundation Services for free?

Yes. A free trial key from the [API Key Portal](https://apikey.llmhub.t-systems.net/) unlocks all models, including Premium models.

## What is the pricing structure?

There are components for LLM and RAG. Shared plans use a monthly token revenue commitment; usage above it is billed at list prices. Plans differ by throughput (TPM and RPM) and by Premium model access. Premium models such as Claude Opus are included on Professional and Agentic, not on Essential. Enterprise is a custom commitment with custom rate limits. For RAG (SmartChat), a monthly flat rate is agreed based on users and storage. See [Plans](/plans).

## What are the rate limits?

Rate limits (TPM and RPM) depend on the selected plan and model. For example, GPT-OSS 120B (T-Cloud, Germany) lists 100 <sup><a href="/reference/rate-limits#about-these-rpm-and-tpm-values">&ast;</a></sup> RPM and 400,000 <sup><a href="/reference/rate-limits#about-these-rpm-and-tpm-values">&ast;</a></sup> input TPM on the Essential plan, scaling up to 300 <sup><a href="/reference/rate-limits#about-these-rpm-and-tpm-values">&ast;</a></sup> RPM and 2,000,000 <sup><a href="/reference/rate-limits#about-these-rpm-and-tpm-values">&ast;</a></sup> input TPM on Agentic. Premium models such as Claude Opus are available on the Professional and Agentic plans.

Published RPM and TPM figures are best-effort ceilings, not part of the [SLA](/reference/service-levels).

See [Rate Limits](/reference/rate-limits) for the full breakdown.

## Can additional LLMs be provided?

Yes, additional models can be hosted on dedicated GPU resources on request. In this case, billing is based on the required GPU resources rather than usage-based. Contact the AIFS team for details.
